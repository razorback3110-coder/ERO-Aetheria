# ERO Audio Pipeline

ERO treats audio as production content, not a placeholder.

## Required categories
- Menu / character creation
- Zone exploration themes for Greenhaven, Everwood, Elyndor, Frostfall, Sunscar, Lunareth, Abyssia and The Eternal Rift
- Dynamic combat layers
- Elite / MVP / World Boss themes
- Dungeon themes
- PvP / GvG themes
- Victory / defeat / transition stingers
- Environmental ambience per biome
- Footsteps, weapons, abilities, impacts, UI and world interactions
- NPC and future voice-over banks

## Runtime
`EROAudioRuntimeDirector` provides state-driven adaptive music. Approved clips are loaded from the ERO audio content namespace and can be replaced without changing gameplay code.

## Legal gate
Every production audio asset must be recorded in the ERO Audio License Registry with source, exact license, commercial-use permission, redistribution/embedding rights, attribution requirement, version/date and verification URL. Unknown or incompatible licenses are rejected.

## Source policy
Prefer CC0/public-domain or explicitly commercial-compatible licenses. Do not extract or reuse music, sounds or voice assets from proprietary games. Original ERO music commissioned/generated for ERO must have documented commercial rights before release.
