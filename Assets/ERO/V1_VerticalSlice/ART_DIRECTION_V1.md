# ERO V1 Art Direction

Target: the approved anime dark-fantasy MMORPG gameplay reference.

Priority order:
1. Character readability and silhouette
2. Strong environment depth and scale
3. Cinematic third-person camera
4. Blue/black/gold ERO interface language
5. Strong magical VFX and Rift lighting
6. Dungeon/boss spectacle
7. Replace procedural placeholders with commercially licensed production assets

Production asset sources must be license-cleared before shipping:
- Unity Asset Store assets: verify the package/provider terms
- Quaternius: use only packs/version covered by its commercial license
- Poly Haven: CC0
- Kenney: CC0
- Noto Sans CJK: OFL-1.1
- Roboto: Apache-2.0

Do not import ripped assets from other games/private servers.
