# ERO — Commercial Asset Source Register
Version: V541
Date: 2026-09-10

This register is a production/legal tracking document, not legal advice.
Before commercial release, every third-party asset actually shipped must have
its license archived and its exact asset/source recorded.

## APPROVED SOURCE CATEGORIES

### Quaternius
Official license: https://quaternius.com/license.html
Use: characters, humanoid rigs, fantasy outfits, props, environments, animations.
Current license states commercial use is permitted, modification is permitted,
no attribution is required, and standalone redistribution of the assets is prohibited.
STATUS: APPROVED FOR INTEGRATION, SUBJECT TO RECORDING THE EXACT PACK/LICENSE VERSION.

### Poly Haven
Official license: https://polyhaven.com/license
Use: HDRIs, PBR textures, materials, selected 3D assets.
Assets are CC0 and may be used commercially. Keep the source URL in the project
record even when attribution is not legally required.
STATUS: APPROVED.

### Kenney
Official support/licensing information: https://kenney.nl/support
Use: UI elements, icons, props and selected game assets.
Kenney states its game assets are public-domain/CC0 and commercially usable.
STATUS: APPROVED, SUBJECT TO ARCHIVING THE EXACT ASSET PAGE/LICENSE FILE.

### OpenGameArt
Official licensing FAQ: https://opengameart.org/node/5571
Use: only individually reviewed assets.
Commercial use can be permitted, but the individual asset license controls
attribution, share-alike and other obligations.
STATUS: REVIEW-REQUIRED PER ASSET.

### Unity Asset Store
Official legal information: https://unity.com/legal
Use: paid/free Unity packages only after checking the package's own EULA,
Asset Store EULA and any additional terms.
STATUS: REVIEW-REQUIRED PER PACKAGE.

### Freesound
Official API terms: https://freesound.org/docs/api/terms_of_use.html
Do not treat the API itself as a blanket commercial license. Individual sound
licenses and the commercial-use terms must be checked before shipping.
STATUS: REVIEW-REQUIRED / DO NOT BULK IMPORT.

## PROHIBITED FOR COMMERCIAL SHIPMENT WITHOUT WRITTEN PERMISSION

- Ripped assets from Ragnarok Online or other commercial games.
- Assets extracted from private servers.
- Datamined client files.
- Copyrighted maps, sprites, models, music or VFX copied from another game.
- Assets with unclear provenance.
- Assets whose license cannot be archived and verified.

## ERO ORIGINAL ASSETS

Any asset created specifically for ERO should be marked:
OWNER: Eternal Realms Online project
RIGHTS: project-owned / verify contributor agreements before commercial release.

## RELEASE RULE

No asset may move from REVIEW-REQUIRED or DO_NOT_SHIP into a release build
until its source and license have been recorded.
