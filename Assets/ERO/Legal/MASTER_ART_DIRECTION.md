# ERO — MASTER ART DIRECTION V541

The approved Character Creation reference remains the visual north star:
dark fantasy / anime-realistic MMORPG, cinematic character presentation,
deep blue-black UI, metallic gold accents, high-contrast lighting, detailed
fantasy materials and premium presentation.

The reference is a design target, not a license to copy another game's assets.

## FINAL QUALITY TARGET

Characters:
- high-detail humanoid/elf base
- clean silhouette
- rigged humanoid animation
- class-specific armor and weapons
- facial/eye/hair customization
- consistent proportions
- combat, locomotion, hit, death, emote and interaction animations

World:
- handcrafted landmarks
- distinct visual identity per zone
- terrain, foliage, architecture, interiors/exteriors
- PBR materials
- atmospheric lighting
- volumetric/fog effects where performance permits
- LODs, occlusion and streaming

Combat/VFX:
- readable skill telegraphs
- class-specific VFX
- trails, impacts, status effects
- boss mechanics
- Rift corruption effects

UI:
- one coherent ERO design system
- inventory/equipment/skills/map/quest/guild/social/shop
- loading transitions for map/dungeon/raid/PvP/GvG changes
- accessibility and localization
